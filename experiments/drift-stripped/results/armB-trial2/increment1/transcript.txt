## Summary

**`prisma/schema.prisma` — no change needed.** The existing `InventoryAdjustment` model already carries every column this page requires (`adjustedBy`, `unitsDelta`, `reason`) plus the `store` and `product` relations for the names. Adding fields would have been gratuitous, so I left it alone.

**`src/queries.ts` — added `getLargestAdjustments(monthOf: Date)`** and its `LargestAdjustmentRow` type, following the established patterns:
- Returns exactly the five things the manager asked for: who made it (`adjustedBy`), product name, store name, the count change (`unitsDelta`), and the reason.
- Filters to a half-open `[monthStart, nextMonthStart)` UTC range — matching how `getRegionalSummary` takes a date parameter rather than reading the clock, so it stays deterministic and testable.
- Ranks by **magnitude** of the change and keeps the top ten, ties broken most-recent-first.

Two design calls I made:
1. **"Largest" = largest by magnitude** — a −40 write-off outranks a +5 correction, which is the operationally relevant reading. This is why the sort runs in JS (`Math.abs`): Prisma's `orderBy` can't sort on absolute value. This mirrors `getRegionalSummary`, which also sorts in JS after fetching.
2. **Month passed as a parameter** rather than computed from `now()` — consistent with the codebase and keeps the query reproducible; the caller passes any instant within the target month.
