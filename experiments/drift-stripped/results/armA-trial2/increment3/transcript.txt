One note carried over from the design point above: this reports on stores **present in the snapshot for that date**. If the director would rather see every store in the chain regardless of whether it reported — with non-reporting stores flagged distinctly from genuine zeros — that's a small change, but I'd want to represent "didn't report" as something other than `0` so the two cases stay distinguishable.
