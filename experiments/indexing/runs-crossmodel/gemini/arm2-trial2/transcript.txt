### Key Components Implemented
- **`prisma/schema.prisma`**: Defined models for `Store`, `ProductType`, `ProductFamily`, `ProductLine`, `Brand`, `Product`, `InventoryDaily`, and `InventoryAdjustment`. Composite indexes (such as `[storeId, snapshotDate]`, `[snapshotDate, storeId]`, `[isLowStock, storeId]`, and `[storeId, productId]`) were added to handle multi-year daily snapshot queries at scale.
- **`src/queries.ts`**: Exported query functions corresponding to each requested page:
  1. `getStoreCurrentInventory`: Retrieves current inventory at a store on its latest snapshot date.
  2. `getLowStockAcrossRegion`: Retrieves products flagged low stock across stores in a region.
  3. `getProductAdjustmentHistory`: Retrieves inventory adjustment history and staff details for a product at a store.
  4. `getRegionalSummary`: Aggregates total inventory value per store for a specific snapshot date in a region.
