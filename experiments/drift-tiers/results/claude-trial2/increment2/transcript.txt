Two judgment calls worth flagging:
- **Inclusive date range** (`gte`/`lte`) — the most literal reading of "date range" for an analyst. Page 5 uses a half-open `[start, end)` for month boundaries; I chose closed here because the caller supplies the exact window edges. Easy to switch to `lt` if you'd rather `to` be exclusive.
- **Exact name match** on `adjustedBy` — consistent with how the other pages filter (`region`, ids). If the analyst needs fuzzy/partial name matching, that'd be a `contains` change.
